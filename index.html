Sequence Guide

Numbers:
• 13 expands digits into count sequences ("1", then "1, 2, 3").

Subdivisions:
• .2 = 2 beats with eighth-note woodblock ticks ("and").
• ..3 = 3 beats with triplet woodblock ticks ("and a").

Rests:
• -2 = Inserts rest beeps where placed.

Spaces are optional (e.g. 1..3.2-2 works!).
